Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RotateIt 3D handles information when you use the app to create, edit, style, and export 3D photo compositions.

Privacy-first photo editing RotateIt 3D is designed to process your photos on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your imported, edited, or exported photos.

1. Information We Access

Photos and Images

RotateIt 3D allows you to select photos from your device using Android's photo selection functionality. Selected photos are processed locally on your device for editing, cropping, rotation, 3D transformation, effects, and export.

Camera

If you choose to capture a photo using the in-app camera, RotateIt 3D requests camera access. Camera access is used to capture images that you choose to edit. We do not continuously access or record your camera.

Device and Technical Information

Third-party services used by the app may process technical information needed to provide their services. Depending on the service, device, consent choices, and configuration, this may include device type, operating system version, app version, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising identifiers, and diagnostic or interaction information.

2. How Your Photos Are Processed

RotateIt 3D is designed to process photos on your device. Editing operations such as 3D rotation, perspective transformation, cropping, scaling, flipping, shadows, backgrounds, borders, glare effects, and image export are performed locally on your Android device.

We do not operate a server that stores your edited or imported photos. Exported images remain under your control and are saved to the location you select on your device.

3. AI 3D Scene Stylist

RotateIt 3D includes an optional AI-assisted feature called AI 3D Scene Stylist. It can use a text prompt to suggest rotation angles, scale, shadow settings, background colors, and other 3D scene settings.

When you use the AI Stylist, the text prompt required to generate a styling suggestion may be sent to Google's Gemini services for processing. The selected photo itself is not sent to Gemini for photo editing or image generation by RotateIt 3D.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in AI prompts. Google's applicable terms and privacy practices may also apply to your use of Gemini services.

4. Advertising

RotateIt 3D uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements that may provide additional in-app credits after an eligible ad is completed.

Depending on your device settings, consent choices, applicable laws, and Google's advertising configuration, AdMob may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, app information, approximate location, and advertising or interaction information.

You can manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings.

5. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

RotateIt 3D offers an optional VIP subscription that may provide benefits such as removal of advertisements and unlimited exports.

Purchases are processed through Google Play, while subscription entitlement management may be handled through RevenueCat. RotateIt 3D does not receive or store your full payment card, bank account, or other payment credentials.

Google Play and RevenueCat may process information necessary to process purchases, verify transactions, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, determine entitlement, and help prevent fraudulent transactions.

6. RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to help manage subscription status and entitlement information. RevenueCat may process information such as app user identifiers, purchase information, subscription status, product information, transaction information, and technical information required to provide its services.

RevenueCat does not receive your payment card details directly from RotateIt 3D.

7. Data Storage on Your Device

RotateIt 3D may store certain application preferences, editing state, drafts, quota information, export preferences, and other app-related settings locally on your device.

Local storage may support features such as auto-saved drafts, editing preferences, export preferences, free-generation and credit information, and application settings.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to our own servers.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share your photos with advertisers.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when required to provide specific application functionality, including:

Google AdMob Advertising and ad measurement.
Google Gemini Optional AI-assisted styling from text prompts.
Google Play Application distribution and purchases.
RevenueCat Subscription and entitlement management.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application. Because RotateIt 3D processes photos locally whenever possible, your photo files are not stored on our servers.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We do not maintain a server-side database containing your personal photos or exported images. Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to their respective policies and practices.

Local application data remains on your device until it is deleted by you, the application is uninstalled, or the relevant data is otherwise removed through your device's settings.

10. Children's Privacy

RotateIt 3D is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action where applicable.

11. Your Choices

You can control your use of the app's permissions through Android settings.

  • Grant or deny camera permission.
  • Control photo access.
  • Manage available advertising personalization settings.
  • Cancel your subscription through Google Play.
  • Uninstall the application.
  • Delete locally stored application data through Android settings.

Some functionality may not work if the required permission is denied.

12. Third-Party Services

RotateIt 3D uses or may use third-party services including Google AdMob, Google Gemini, Google Play, and RevenueCat.

These services may collect or process information according to their own applicable privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of these providers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, our services, legal requirements, or third-party integrations.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy. You should periodically review this page for the latest information.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or RotateIt 3D's privacy practices, please contact us.
